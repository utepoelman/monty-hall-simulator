💡 Why Switching Doubles Your Odds

The Monty Hall Problem is a famous counter-intuitive probability puzzle based on the American television game show Let's Make a Deal.

1. Your Initial Pick (1/3 Chance)

When you first pick a door, there is a 1 in 3 (33.3%) chance you picked the car, and a 2 in 3 (66.7%) chance the car is behind one of the other two doors.

2. The Host's Revealed Door

The host (Monty Hall) knows what is behind every door. He must always open an unselected door that contains a Goat. He never opens the car!

3. The Magic of Switching (2/3 Chance)

Because the host filtered out a Goat, the 66.7% probability that the car was in the "other group" now concentrates entirely onto the single remaining unopened door! Switching converts every initial loss into a win.
